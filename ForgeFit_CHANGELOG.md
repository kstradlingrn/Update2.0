# ForgeFit update changes

## This update — October 10, 2026
- Embedded start/action exercise illustrations in both views, including separate components for combination exercises.
- Coverage for all 55 current exercise entries and 24 legacy exercise entries still usable in saved workouts.
- Woman, Man, and Drill Sergeant spoken coach options, with step-by-step instruction and key technique points.
- Replay, Stop, optional speech on the next exercise, and per-coach device voice selection.
- Renamed Coach View to Single View; explicit Single View and Full Plan buttons.
- Exercise alternatives can work other muscles, with same-muscle choices presented first.
- Alternative workout generation with Undo.
- Retained real-photo and video links alongside built-in illustrations.

## Features carried forward from the supplied previous app source
This is an inventory of the prior source, not an assertion that each feature was introduced in the same historical release.

- 50 combination strength exercises; one repetition completes the full movement sequence.
- Strength-only, cardio-only, and combined workouts.
- Daily readiness based on energy and soreness.
- Build workouts by exercise count or time; adjustable rest and rep pacing.
- Edit workout, add exercise, Undo, and repeat the last workout.
- Separate Home, Apartment Gym, and Outdoors equipment choices; allowed-position controls and exercise exclusions.
- Head-above-heart and elevated-head position preferences.
- Exercise preference choices: more, less, neutral, or never.
- Strength progression, muscle recovery, history, and progress tracking.
- Food substitutions, craving options, restaurant guidance, and portion-scaled nutrition estimates.
- Workout-data export and persistent local workout storage.
- Links to online real exercise photos and videos.
